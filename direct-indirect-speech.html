📰 Crime Scene Report

A witness spoke to reporters after the incident.

"I have never seen anything like that in my life."

Your task is to report what the witness said.

Direct Speech

"I have never seen anything like that in my life."

Indirect Speech

The bystander said that he had never seen anything like that in his life.

Why Journalists Use Reported Speech

  • It sounds formal.
  • It summarises what someone said.
  • Quotation marks disappear.
  • The tense usually shifts backwards.

Quick Quiz

Which sentence uses correct reported speech?

A) He said, "I saw the crime."

B) He said that he saw the crime.

C) He said that he had seen the crime.

Headline Builder

Turn this into reported speech:

"I heard a loud noise," the neighbour said.

Answer: The neighbour said that he had heard a loud noise.
